2. CORE PROJECT INNOVATION
==================================================

AarogyaMitra AI is not merely a chatbot that answers health questions.

Its core innovation is an adaptive learning loop:

Citizen asks a health question
        ↓
Understand intent
        ↓
Retrieve trusted health information
        ↓
Generate simple explanation
        ↓
Check understanding
        ↓
Detect misconception
        ↓
Adapt explanation
        ↓
Teach-Back
        ↓
Measure understanding
        ↓
Update learning profile
        ↓
Reinforce later

Important concepts:

AHLP
----
Adaptive Health Learning Profile.

The system maintains a learning-oriented profile based on demonstrated interaction/understanding, topic history, misconceptions and reinforcement needs.

MISCONCEPTION ENGINE
--------------------
Identifies incorrect or potentially harmful health beliefs and supports correction.

ADAPTIVE TEACHING
-----------------
The system changes the explanation according to the user's demonstrated understanding.

TEACH-BACK
----------
The system asks the user to explain or answer something to determine whether the user actually understood the health information.

HUMAN UNDERSTANDING SCORE
-------------------------
Measures actual demonstrated understanding instead of merely measuring whether an answer was delivered.

RETENTION / REINFORCEMENT
-------------------------
Previously learned information can be reinforced later.

HRRS
----
Health Response Reliability/Safety scoring concept used to support response reliability and conservative escalation.

MEDICAL SAFETY LAYER
--------------------
Uses rules + LLM validation + risk handling.

PHIX
----
Public-health intelligence layer that uses anonymized/aggregated citizen interaction signals for government intelligence.

The project goal is to achieve at least 80% measured accuracy on the defined health-query benchmark.

Do not simply claim "80% accuracy." The evaluation system must actually measure it.
